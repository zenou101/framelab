## 5. Accuracy of hardware monitoring

Performance counters, sensors, frame timings and derived statistics are **approximations supplied by
Windows, device drivers and third-party components**. They can be wrong, delayed, rounded, missing or
misleading. In particular:

- utilisation, clock and memory figures are sampled at intervals and are not instantaneous truths;
- frame timings depend on the operating system's presentation events and may not correspond exactly
  to what a person perceives on screen;
- the same machine may report different values under different drivers, power plans, virtualisation,
  remote sessions, hybrid graphics configurations or after a Windows update;
- "displayed" and "presented" frame cadences are distinct measurements and are reported separately
  precisely because they can disagree; and
- values a system does not expose are shown as unavailable, which is not evidence that the underlying
  resource is unused or healthy.

FrameLab's analysis features produce **heuristic observations**, not findings of fact or causes. A
reported observation may be a false positive; the absence of an observation is not proof that no
problem exists.
